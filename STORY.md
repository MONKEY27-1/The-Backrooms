\# THE BACKROOMS: UNCHARTED REALITY

\## Main Story — Story Bible, Version 1



\### 1. The premise



Humanity did not create the Backrooms.



Long before humans existed, an ancient civilization discovered an immensely powerful presence. This presence possessed the ability to create and manipulate an entire reality.



That reality became what humanity would eventually call the Backrooms.



The ancient civilization eventually discovered that the presence was dangerous. During a catastrophic event that destroyed portions of the Backrooms, the civilization managed to seal the presence inside an unrecognizable artifact.



The artifact was hidden somewhere within the Backrooms.



The ancient civilization eventually disappeared. The artifact remained.



Millions of years later, humans accidentally discovered the Backrooms.



\### 2. Obsidian Research Division



\*\*Obsidian Research Division (ORD)\*\* is a relatively young research organization that discovered the Backrooms during an unrelated experimental project.



ORD initially saw the Backrooms as an unprecedented scientific opportunity.



It established research stations, conducted experiments, studied entities, and investigated the unusual properties of the environment.



However, ORD gradually became increasingly secretive.



Its researchers began conducting experiments on people trapped inside the Backrooms. Some experiments involved deliberately sacrificing people to study their effects on the environment.



ORD also discovered that people could sometimes escape.



It decided that allowing people to leave was too dangerous for its research and its secrecy.



Anyone attempting to escape could be captured or eliminated.



ORD's personnel are separated into ranks. Lower-ranking employees receive incomplete information, while senior personnel conceal the organization's most important discoveries.



ORD does not fully understand the presence or the artifact.



\### 3. The protagonist



The protagonist is an ORD employee assigned to a classified experiment.



They wear a hazmat suit and have little personal background. Their identity and name can be established separately.



The protagonist is instructed to enter Level 0, recover an unknown object, and document the condition of several deceased individuals.



They are told very little about the assignment.



The organization initially believes the deaths were caused by an entity.



The protagonist is not told that the deceased individuals were former ORD employees.



\### 4. The artifact



While exploring Level 0, the protagonist discovers several dead employees wearing identical hazmat suits.



Near their bodies is an object that cannot be identified or classified.



When the protagonist touches it, they experience an immediate blackout.



The artifact disappears into their body.



The protagonist wakes up and continues the mission, initially unaware of what happened.



The artifact has several unusual properties:



\- It allows the protagonist to return after death.

\- Each return places them in an almost identical version of the Backrooms.

\- The protagonist remembers previous versions.

\- Small environmental differences appear after each return.

\- Other people and objects may be different between versions.



However, the artifact has a terrible hidden cost.



Whenever the protagonist dies, another person dies in their place.



The victim might be an ordinary person in the real world, an explorer in the Backrooms, or someone connected to ORD.



The protagonist initially has no idea that this is happening.



The full truth is uncovered near the end of the campaign.



\### 5. The mysterious presence



The presence is the original creator of the Backrooms.



It was sealed inside the artifact by an ancient civilization.



Although imprisoned, it can still influence parts of the Backrooms.



It subtly manipulates the environment, sends strange signals, and occasionally uses voices belonging to people the protagonist recognizes.



It appears to help the protagonist.



It wants the protagonist to reach the original prison where it was sealed.



The protagonist believes these strange occurrences are either unusual properties of the Backrooms or signs that something is trying to guide them.



The presence is actually manipulating their journey.



Its objective is to escape the artifact and enter the real world.



It wants revenge against humanity and intends to spread the Backrooms into reality.



Its physical appearance is a constantly changing, multicolored biomass. When it finally reveals itself near the end, it takes on a human form.



\### 6. Campaign structure



The campaign is mostly linear, but levels allow open exploration.



There is no central hub. The player travels between levels, discovering exits, completing objectives, and investigating the environment.



The campaign should take approximately 8–12 hours or less.



The initial level order is a proposal and can change as more levels are developed.



\#### Prologue — The Assignment



The game opens with a cinematic briefing inside an ORD facility.



The protagonist is assigned a classified recovery mission.



The briefing is deliberately incomplete. The protagonist is told to locate an unknown object and document the deceased employees found in the area.



The player enters Level 0.



\#### Chapter 1 — Level 0: Threshold



The protagonist begins exploring the yellow office environment.



They discover the bodies of former ORD employees.



The player investigates the area, documenting the bodies and searching for the requested object.



Eventually, the artifact is discovered.



Touching it causes the protagonist to black out.



They awaken and continue exploring.



Strange environmental events begin occurring, but nothing clearly explains them.



\#### Chapter 2 — Level 0.2: Remodeled Mess



The protagonist reaches a distorted extension of the original environment.



The level introduces the idea that the Backrooms can contain spaces that feel familiar but behave differently.



The protagonist finds evidence of previous ORD activity.



Some records suggest that the organization has conducted more experiments than the protagonist was told.



\#### Chapter 3 — Level 0.3: The Icy Rooms



The protagonist enters an environment that is significantly different from the previous levels.



The cold conditions introduce additional survival challenges.



The player discovers more evidence that ORD has been studying unusual properties of the Backrooms.



Strange signals begin appearing on the protagonist's equipment.



\#### Chapter 4 — Level 1: Habitable Zone



The protagonist encounters evidence of other explorers and previous expeditions.



They discover abandoned supplies, records, and signs of people attempting to escape.



ORD communications suggest that the organization has been tracking escape attempts.



The protagonist begins questioning the purpose of their mission.



\#### Chapter 5 — Level 2: Pipe Dreams



The protagonist navigates the industrial tunnels.



This chapter introduces more dangerous exploration and environmental hazards.



A hidden ORD research outpost reveals that the organization has been conducting experiments on people and entities.



The protagonist discovers that some employees have been ordered to conceal information from lower-ranking personnel.



\#### Chapter 6 — Level 19: Attic Floorboards



The protagonist discovers evidence that appears to connect to the ancient history of the Backrooms.



Some of the architecture and objects do not appear to belong to any human civilization.



The presence begins manipulating the environment more noticeably.



It still appears helpful.



\#### Chapter 7 — Level 37: Sublimity (Poolrooms)



The protagonist explores the enormous pool environment.



The atmosphere is quieter and more isolated.



The player discovers strange recordings and evidence of previous explorers.



Some of the recordings contain voices that should not be able to communicate with the protagonist.



The presence becomes a more noticeable part of the journey.



\#### Chapter 8 — Level Fun: =)



The protagonist enters an environment that initially appears unusually welcoming.



However, the environment becomes increasingly suspicious.



The player discovers that not everything encountered in the Backrooms is necessarily human.



This chapter reinforces the organization's uncertainty about the nature of the inhabitants.



It also introduces evidence that ORD has deliberately hidden the truth about certain locations.



\#### Chapter 9 — Level 188 Å: The Courtyard of Windows



The protagonist explores a strange architectural environment.



The player begins finding evidence of the ancient civilization responsible for imprisoning the presence.



The protagonist learns that the artifact is not simply an unusual object.



It is connected to the origin of the Backrooms.



The presence begins guiding the protagonist toward the original prison.



\#### Chapter 10 — Level -1: The Data Center



The protagonist discovers the massive data center.



This level contains the largest ORD research facility in the Backrooms.



The facility contains extensive research records, experiments, personnel files, and information about the organization's activities.



The protagonist discovers evidence of people being sacrificed in experiments.



They also uncover information about the artifact and the ancient civilization.



The organization becomes aware that the protagonist possesses the artifact.



ORD attempts to capture the protagonist and recover it.



The protagonist finally discovers that the presence has been manipulating their journey.



\#### Chapter 11 — Level 3: Electrical Station

The protagonist enters the deafening industrial labyrinth of Level 3.

Sprawling maintenance corridors, high-voltage transformer banks, and massive generators line the cramped brick pathways.

ORD established automated power-siphoning outposts here to energize deep-level expedition equipment.

In the wreckage of an abandoned ORD transformer station, the protagonist recovers encrypted maintenance logs.

The logs reveal that the level's rushing electrical grid was never installed by humans—it is drawing enormous surges of residual energy directly from the ancient seal.



\#### Chapter 12 — Level 4: Abandoned Office

Escaping the electrical roar, the protagonist emerges into the eerie, tranquil quiet of Level 4.

Endless rows of pristine white office cubicles, conference tables, and water coolers stretch into the distance beneath soft fluorescent lighting.

ORD previously utilized this sector as a major forward base and supply hub due to the near-total absence of hostile entities and the abundance of almond water.

However, the protagonist discovers that the ORD outpost was hastily evacuated.

Scattered memos and barricaded doors document that personnel began suffering from window-induced anomalies, warning that the dark exterior panes lead only to bottomless voids.



\#### Chapter 13 — Level 5: The Hotel

Following an antique staircase, the protagonist arrives in an opulent, infinite 1920s hotel complex.

Polished wood wainscoting, grand chandeliers, and endless carpeted hallways lined with numbered guest suites define the environment.

Faint, distant phonograph jazz and phantom whispers echo down the vacant corridors, eroding the explorer's mental stability.

In the sweltering heat of the subterranean boiler room, the protagonist finds the remnants of an ORD containment unit.

Research notes confirm that ORD attempted to isolate an intelligent humanoid entity inhabiting the suites, and that anomalous spatial tears in the hotel lead directly into lower subterranean levels.



\#### Chapter 14 — Level 6: Lights Out

A rusted metal hatch in the boiler room drops the protagonist into an abyss of total, suffocating darkness.

In Level 6, natural light does not exist, and artificial illumination from flashlights is unnaturally smothered and restricted to a few feet.

The endless, smooth concrete corridors are acoustically dead, inducing severe sensory deprivation within minutes.

ORD expedition records left at the entrance detail that an entire research detachment suffered irreversible psychological degradation after losing their bearings in the pitch black.

In the silence, the ancient presence communicates directly with the protagonist, guiding their steps through the darkness toward a descending staircase.



\#### Chapter 15 — Level 7: Thalassophobia

The staircase descends into a damp concrete bunker that opens directly onto an impossible, boundless dark ocean.

A low, concrete ceiling hangs oppressive over freezing black water that stretches into the infinite distance.

Submerged corridors, submerged structures, and sudden oceanic drop-offs require dangerous aquatic traversal.

Exploring sunken ORD dive stations, the protagonist discovers that the water level fluctuates anomalously.

At the bottom of a flooded chasm, a submerged cavern entrance marks the only viable route deeper into the earth.



\#### Chapter 16 — Level 8: Cave System

Emerging from the flooded passage, the protagonist enters a vast, pitch-black karst cavern system.

Jagged limestone formations, towering stalactites, and narrow, claustrophobic crawlways twist through the bedrock with non-Euclidean geometry.

Every sound echoes into the dark, attracting predatory entities that stalk the subterranean chambers.

The protagonist finds the discarded survival gear and torn survey maps of an ORD deep-reconnaissance unit.

As the cavern floor begins to destabilize, the rock gives way beneath the protagonist's feet, sending them falling into the darkness below.



\#### Chapter 17 — Level 9: Darkened Suburbs

The protagonist crashes onto the wet asphalt of an endless suburban neighborhood frozen in perpetual midnight.

Rows of dark, vacant houses with manicured lawns and concrete driveways sit beneath the warm amber glow of flickering sodium-vapor streetlights.

A dense, low-hanging fog rolls across the streets, obscuring long-range visibility.

Searchlight beams sweep across the neighborhood from anomalous airborne entities patrolling the roads.

Using the houses and yards for concealment, the protagonist navigates the neighborhood, gathering the final ORD research coordinates that pinpoint the ancient prison.



\### 7. The final chapter — The Original Prison



The protagonist reaches an entirely original level containing the ancient prison.



The environment is almost impossible to understand.



It combines enormous unnatural structures with strange biological architecture.



The remains of the ancient civilization are scattered throughout the area.



The protagonist discovers the original sealing mechanism and learns the full truth about the presence.



The presence finally appears in physical form.



Instead of the constantly changing biomass previously encountered, it takes on a human form.



ORD eventually captures the protagonist and attempts to recover the artifact.



However, the organization has underestimated what it has been dealing with.



The protagonist must decide what happens next.



\### 8. Ending One — Release



The protagonist chooses to destroy ORD.



The organization loses control of its facilities and its research.



The protagonist eventually finds a way into the real world.



However, destroying ORD also destroys the remaining containment system.



The presence escapes.



The Backrooms begin spreading into reality.



Reality begins breaking down, and humanity is exposed to something it cannot understand.



The protagonist successfully escapes the organization, but unknowingly releases the greatest threat humanity has ever faced.



\### 9. Ending Two — Containment



The protagonist chooses to stop the presence.



They prevent its escape, even though doing so means remaining trapped in the Backrooms.



The organization may survive, but the presence remains imprisoned.



The protagonist is left inside the Backrooms with the knowledge of what almost happened.



The ending is intentionally bittersweet.



\### 10. Future content



The campaign will eventually expand with:



\- Additional established Backrooms levels.

\- Completely original levels.

\- Secret levels.

\- Optional story discoveries.

\- Additional ORD facilities.

\- Hidden recordings and documents.

\- More information about the ancient civilization.

\- Secret endings.

\- Further discoveries about the artifact's victims.



Not every level needs to advance the main story directly. Some levels should exist primarily for exploration, atmosphere, survival, and discovery.



The central mystery should remain consistent throughout all future expansions.
